# Analyst Notes: Hypotheses and Observations

> **\*All 2026 figures are UNOFFICIAL.** Nebraska Secretary of State (SOS) results site, "Unofficial Results," county data re-downloaded Nov 27, 2026 10:21–10:24 AM CT (1,322 of 1,322 precincts; 779,787 ballots cast). The State Board of Canvassers meets Nov 30, 2026 at 11:30 AM CT; numbers can still change. Registration: SOS VR Statistics Count Report processed Nov 1, 2026. **Treating the removed votes as injected votes is the analyst's hypothesis.** The arithmetic measures a pattern. It cannot test cause, and nothing here is proof of wrongdoing. PRIVATE working copy: not for publication.

> **Every item on this page is a HYPOTHESIS or an OBSERVATION, not a proven fact.** The Cedar County Cast Vote Record (CVR) has been requested and has not been produced, so none of these can be tested at the ballot level. All remain unverified pending CVR access. Data added under each item was checked in both verification passes (0 mismatches).

## 1. CD3 targeting: HYPOTHESIS

**Hypothesis.** The analyst, working from Cedar County in Nebraska's 3rd Congressional District, hypothesizes that injection targeting shifted to CD3 this cycle. Reasoning: CD3 is Nebraska's Republican stronghold ("red wall") and the state's largest Republican push, so resources may have been redirected there, specifically into mail-in and absentee ballots, after other areas were saturated.

**What the county data shows (Governor, 2026\*, county-level CD assignment).**

| District | Counties | Mean distance from line* | Mean ΔG 2022→2026* | Mean ΔG 2018→2022 | Mean ΔG 2020→2024 | Removal votes* | Removal as % of district ballots* |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CD1 | 11 | +2.91 | +9.55 | -2.93 | +2.90 | 33,835 | 11.80% |
| CD2 | 2 | +5.76 | +7.58 | -2.24 | +2.85 | 24,373 | 10.20% |
| CD3 | 80 | -0.54 | +5.71 | -2.43 | +2.87 | 10,830 | 4.26% |

Observation: on these county-level measures, CD3 counties do **not** sit higher than CD1 or CD2 in 2026\*. CD3's mean distance from the line is slightly negative, and its mean ΔG (+5.71\*) is positive but below CD1 and CD2. In the two earlier cycle pairs the three districts moved almost identically. These are county averages; they cannot see a shift that happens inside counties or inside the mail-ballot stream. That needs the CVR.

## 2. Cedar County and all-mail counties: HYPOTHESIS

**Context.** The analyst runs this analysis from Cedar County, one of Nebraska's 11 all-mail counties (Boone, Cedar, Cherry, Clay, Dawes, Dixon, Garden, Knox, Merrick, Morrill, Stanton).

**Hypothesis.** Injection in CD3 was carried out through ballot harvesting or ballot manipulation, particularly in absentee and all-mail counties and in small outlier counties. Unverified: the Cedar County CVR has been requested and not received.

**Observation (data).** Governor ΔG 2022→2026\*: the 11 all-mail counties average **+5.98\***; the other 82 counties average **+6.23\***. The earlier finding (+5.98 vs +6.23) still holds on today's numbers. All-mail counties moved about the same as everyone else. In the 2026\* level fit, Cedar is zeroed (below the Governor line).

## 3. Slope / saturation theory: HYPOTHESIS

**Hypothesis.** In the 16 counties that earlier showed the opposite direction, Democratic registration was effectively exhausted ("ran out of Democrats"), so the fitted line grew too steep, and injection then expanded into CD3 and small outlier counties to compensate.

**Which 16 counties.** In the data, the 16 are the counties whose ΔG was **positive from 2018 to 2022** (Governor Krist 2018 → Governor Blood 2022, ballots denominator) while the statewide county mean was -2.49. Largest to smallest: Custer, Hamilton, Richardson, Boone, Nuckolls, Chase, Nance, Harlan, Sherman, Brown, Frontier, Greeley, Rock, Keya Paha, Hooker, Logan. All 16 are CD3 counties; together they have 61,136 people and had 6,901 registered Democrats in 2022.

**Observations (data).**
- Their 2022 Democratic registration share averaged 14.45%, against 16.87% in the other 77 counties: lower, but not exhausted.
- Mean ΔG 2018→2022 in the 16: +2.29.
- In 2026\* they sit below the Governor line on average (-2.00, vs +0.42 elsewhere), their 2022→2026\* ΔG averages +4.14 (vs +6.63), and 4 of them are zeroed (Greeley, Logan, Nuckolls, Sherman).
- The fitted Governor/top-ticket multiplier went from -13.08 (2022) to +12.27 (2024) to +27.38\* (2026): the line did steepen.

Whether the steeper line is caused by exhausted registration is the hypothesis; the county data cannot test it.

## 4. Nonpartisan, Libertarian and Republican shares: OBSERVATION + HYPOTHESIS

**Observation (as stated by the analyst).** Nonpartisan and Libertarian vote shares appear to decline in the same counties.

**Hypothesis.** As those pools were used up, injection began drawing from Republican votes as well.

**What can be checked.** Vote shares by party affiliation are not public: ballots do not record the voter's party, and there was **no Libertarian Party candidate** in any 2026 statewide race (parties on the statewide ballot: REP, DEM, Legal Marijuana NOW, Nebraska Working People, America First, petition). So the *vote-share* part of this observation **cannot be verified** from SOS results; it would need the voter-history file or the CVR. What can be checked is **registration share**, Nov 1, 2022 → Nov 1, 2026 (SOS VR reports), in points:

| Group | Counties | Democratic | Republican | Libertarian | Nonpartisan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All 93 counties | 93 | -2.24 | +1.87 | +0.01 | +0.06 |
| The 16 (above) | 16 | -2.58 | +2.58 | -0.03 | -0.20 |
| CD3 | 80 | -2.26 | +1.91 | +0.03 | +0.03 |
| CD1 + CD2 | 13 | -2.15 | +1.60 | -0.12 | +0.26 |
| All-mail 11 | 11 | -2.48 | +2.68 | +0.02 | -0.46 |
| Top 10 by 2026* Governor distance from line | 10 | -1.54 | +0.59 | -0.08 | +0.53 |

Observations (registration, not votes):
- Nonpartisan registration share fell in 40 of 93 counties and Libertarian in 52; statewide county averages barely moved (NP +0.06, Lib +0.01). In the 16 counties and the all-mail counties, NP share fell on average (-0.20 and -0.46).
- Republican registration share **rose** in 85 of 93 counties (average +1.87).
- Across counties, a larger 2026\* Governor distance from the line goes with a **smaller** Republican registration gain (correlation -0.53); the link to NP change (+0.18) and Libertarian change (-0.13) is weak.
